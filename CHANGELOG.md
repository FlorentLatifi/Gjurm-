### Added
- **Six more outlets: 24.** The *Source validation* run from GitHub's runners (2026-11-18) read A2 CNN's, KosovaPress's and Klan Kosova's sitemaps (1,353, 230 and 329 headlines), Gazeta Express's RSS again (403 since 2 Nov), ATA's RSS (403 in October) and Monitor's `/rss` (robots.txt disallows `/feed/` only): all enabled. The new outlets' perspective is *unrated* until the January review (RATINGS.md). Ekonomia Online's feed moved back to `/feed/` (`/sq/rss.xml` answers 404).
- **News sitemaps as feeds.** Some large outlets publish no RSS, or answer RSS requests with 403, but give search engines a sitemap with each article's headline and time: Google News sitemaps (`<news:title>`, `<news:publication_date>`) and PageMap ones (`headline`, `publication_date`). Such a sitemap is now read like a feed (headline, link, time, photo; never text), parsed without expanding entities; a sitemap without headlines is not a feed. A feed address may hold `{year}` and `{month}` for outlets with one sitemap a month. Klan Kosova (`/sitemap/news.xml`, 327 headlines in 2 days), KosovaPress (`/news-sitemap.xml`, 231) and A2 CNN (`/sitemaps/sitemap-{year}-{month}.xml`, 1,353 this month) now point at theirs; they stay disabled until the *Source validation* run from GitHub's runners passes and each is rated.
- **Launch check:** an outlet without a perspective rating is a warning naming it, not a failure ("unrated" is a rating's honest default, ADR-021); no rating at all still fails.
- **A summary of each story** ([ADR-035](docs/DECISIONS.md#adr-035-a-summary-of-each-story-from-all-its-outlets-reports)). On 18 Nov two of the front page's 30 most covered stories had a summary: the site showed the lead article's, which a free model's quota reached for a third of the articles. A new SUMMARIZE stage, before ENRICH, writes 2–3 neutral sentences in Albanian and English for every story of 2+ outlets from the last 3 days, from all its outlets' headlines and opening lines: the most covered first, again after 3 more outlets join. One request per story (≈ 250 a day), counted in the model's daily quota with the fallback model taking over on a 429. Names and numbers the reports do not contain make the summary rejected, not stored. The API adds `summary_scope` (`story` / `article`) and `summary_outlets`; the story page says it is a model's summary of N outlets' reports that can be wrong. Settings: `STORY_SUMMARIES`, `STORY_SUMMARY_MAX_PER_RUN` (20), `STORY_SUMMARY_DAYS` (3), `STORY_SUMMARY_MIN_OUTLETS` (2), `STORY_SUMMARY_REGROW_OUTLETS` (3), `STORY_SUMMARY_MAX_SECONDS` (180), `STORY_SUMMARY_WITH_PAID_MODEL` (off). Migration 0009 adds the summary columns to `core.stories`.

### Fixed (deduplication, 2026-11-18)
- **One outlet's edited headline counted as another article.** When an outlet edits a headline and its feed lists the edit as a new entry (new URL and guid), each version was stored: KOHA's "Zulfaj e quan skandaloze / skandaloz / skandal …" were three articles of story 15523, Zëri's "Kënga nacionalisteserbe / nacionaliste serbe …" two, and *Compare headlines* repeated them. A new dedup layer, **L3b**, folds them in the PROCESS stage: same outlet, within ±3 h, at most two words changed, each a typo, a changed ending, words split or joined, a particle, a label or one word added. A changed number, count or ordinal ("3 / 5 të vdekur", "Pjesa 1 / 2", "e parë / e dytë"), a negation, a day ("sot", "nesër", "sonte") or a live marker ("LIVE", "minutë pas minute") always keeps two articles apart. The edit is stored as a hidden row linked by `duplicate_of_id` to the article shown, which takes the newest headline and keeps its URL, time and analysis; the edit is not analysed. Calibrated on the public archive (34,581 articles, 16 Oct – 18 Nov): 101 articles of 13 outlets fold into 100, every pair checked by hand, all already in their original's story ([ADR-034](docs/DECISIONS.md#adr-034-one-outlets-edited-headline-is-a-revision-not-a-new-article-dedup-layer-l3b)).
- **`gjurme dedup-revisions`** (report; `--apply` to change) folds the edits stored before; on the free path the *Free hosting* workflow's tasks `revisions-report` and `revisions-fold` ([OPERATIONS.md](docs/OPERATIONS.md#fold-edited-headlines-stored-twice)). Nothing is deleted; an edit can be unhidden.

### Changed (analysis queue, 2026-11-18)
- **A free model's quota goes to the news readers see.** On the free path the models analyse about a third of the articles and the keyword rules the rest, provisionally (`ENRICH_RULES_UPGRADE_HOURS`), while the front page, the blindspots and the sitemap show only stories of two or more outlets. Of the articles that wait for a better analysis, the model now takes first those of a story on the front page (2+ outlets, started in the last 3 local days), and in each such story first the article whose headline and summary the story shows; then the others, newest first. Articles without any analysis still go before all of them, and the daily quota per run (ADR-033) is unchanged. The run's stats count them (`queued_front_page`). Stories older than 3 days are not favoured, so a *reanalyse* cannot take today's quota for old news.
- **The banner says how the front page was analysed.** `GET /api/v1/status` → `analysis.front_page` gives, for the stories of 2+ outlets of the last 3 days, how many there are, how many of their articles have an analysis, the share of those a model analysed (`ai_share`) and the share of the stories whose headline comes from a model (`headline_ai_share`). In mixed mode the banner on every page now reads e.g. *"87% of the front page's articles were analysed by an AI model, the rest by keyword rules, as were 66% of all recent articles"* (rounded down; the rules are always named), and the About page (both languages) states both front-page figures.

### Fixed (launch review follow-up, 2026-11-18)
- **"Persons" that were places, institutions or fragments.** The keyword rules (two thirds of the articles on the free path) stored capitalised runs joined by "i", "e", "të" as people: "Vitomiricë të Pejës" (a village, 460 articles), "i Kosovës" (349), "Shtetet e Bashkuara" (the United States, 205), "Kuvendit të Kosovës" (201), "Dhomat e Specializuara" (232). Albanian personal names have no such word. The rules now drop the connector a removed role leaves ("Presidentja e Kosovës" → "Kosovës"), know organisations by their first word in any case ("Kuvendit", "Lidhjes", "Dogana", "Qendrën"), places of several words ("Shtetet e Bashkuara", "Mali i Zi", "Lindja e Mesme"), a village of a town ("… të Pejës"), more countries, acronyms in a case ("i LDK-së" → LDK) and more roles ("Ish-presidentja", "Sulmuesi"); any other name with a connector is an organisation, never a person. A model's "person" with a connector is read the same way (`entities_not_person`). `gjurme entities-cleanup` reads the stored ones again (report: `not_persons`), moves their links to what they are, and deletes 19 fragments such as "e Republikës". Checked against the 1,600 most-mentioned live names: every one of the 27 that become places is a place.
- **A genitive after "i", "e", "të" keeps its ending when case forms merge.** The live merge named "Vitomiricë të Pejës" "Vitomiricë të Pejë" and "Maqedonia e Veriut" "Maqedonia e Veriu": the nominative preference cut the last word, which grammar requires in the genitive there. Such names now take the form used most.
- **More outlets naming themselves:** "Gazeta Sinjali" (148 of Sinjali's own articles, as a person) and "Gazeta Online Reporteri.net" are aliases in `sources.yaml`.
- `entities-cleanup --apply` from the CLI is tested to commit, and applies its changes in a few statements for all entities instead of three per entity: on live data the per-entity version ran into the workflow's 25-minute limit (statements from a GitHub runner to Neon are round trips). Its transaction allows statements up to 10 minutes.

### Fixed (launch review, 2026-11-18)
- **Search engines saw every story page empty.** The site is a single-page app that reads its content from `/api/v1/`, and `robots.txt` disallowed `/api/`: Googlebot and Bingbot, which render pages, may not fetch what robots.txt blocks, so the 5,000 story pages in the sitemap rendered without a headline or outlets. `robots.txt` now allows `/api/v1/` (the admin API, the docs and the share pages stay closed; the longest rule wins) and every API answer carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`, so the JSON is fetched but never listed. The *Launch check* verifies both.
- **The status pill was yellow ("degraded") on every page since Gazeta Express was disabled.** An outlet switched off in `sources.yaml` kept its last verification status, `failing`, and the public status counted it as a lost source. Only a source the pipeline switched off after failing in a row makes the status `degraded` now.
- **One entity per person or place.** On the live site "Kosovës" (2,474 articles), "Kosova" (2,316), "Kosovë" (1,313) and "Kosovën" (525) were four places, "Hashim Thaçi" and "Hashim Thaçit" two people, and "Gazeta Online Reporteri", the line Reporteri.net's feed ends every excerpt with, the third most-mentioned *person* (1,444 articles). New analyses now link a case form of a person's or place's name to the entity already stored (the same words but the last, which differs by a case ending only; organisations are left alone), and drop an outlet's own name or alias (`aliases` in `sources.yaml`) from its own articles, flagged `entities_self_reference`. `gjurme entities-cleanup` (report) and `--apply` clean what is already stored, keeping the most-linked entity under its nominative name ("Kosova", "Hashim Thaçi"); on the free path, the *Free hosting* workflow's tasks `entities-report` and `entities-merge` ([OPERATIONS.md](docs/OPERATIONS.md#clean-up-entities-outlets-own-names-case-forms)).
- **The current Claude models are priced.** `claude-haiku-5-5`, `claude-sonnet-5-5` and `claude-fable-5-2` were missing from `pricing.py`, so the budget guard priced them like the most expensive model: Haiku 5.5 at 100 times its price, which would have stopped the analysis after a few articles a day. Haiku 5.5 is asked not to think (it does by default) and Sonnet 5.5 gets `thinking: between_tools` (it refuses `disabled`). At ≈ 1,900 articles a day Haiku 5.5 would cost ≈ $12 a month ([AI_ENRICHMENT.md](docs/AI_ENRICHMENT.md#cost-model)); its quality on Albanian names is still to be checked.

### Added
- **Zëri is read twice a run.** Its feed lists 10 items and has no next page; on 17 Nov it was all new between two runs 4 times. The *Source validation* report, which now looks for more of a short feed without a page 2 (`?limit=50` and similar, and the other feeds the site links), found no longer variant and no other feed. So a source whose feed overflowed one run after another in the last 24 hours without catching up is now read a second time at the end of each run (`INGEST_SECOND_READ`, on by default; `INGEST_SECOND_READ_AFTER_MINUTES`, 3): the time in which its items can fall out of the feed unread is the rest of the interval, not all of it. No extra run and no extra database wake-up; its items reach the site with the next run.

### Fixed
- **Every warning on a run now points at a real problem, and says what and where.** In November's live runs almost every run warned about `source_freshness_6h` and `feed_overflow_24h`, failed `enrichment_backlog_6h` (and alerted it every 6 hours), and many ended `partial`, while the 30-minute runs were regular. From the runs' logs (13–17 Nov) and the *Source validation* report:
  - `enrichment_backlog_6h` counted the articles the workflow's *reanalyse* (`enrich-requeue --provider fake`) put back for a model on purpose: thousands, each with the keyword rules' analysis, waiting for days by design under a free quota. It now counts only articles with **no** analysis 6 h after arriving; the others are in `details.awaiting_better_analysis`.
  - `enrichment_failure_rate_24h` left 429s out already (since 13 Nov), but counted the keyword rules' results as successful calls, so a model failing every other call read as 5 %. It now measures model calls only, by model and error kind (`details.failed_by`), and only from 20 answered calls a day: three calls before a quota ran out make no rate.
  - `feed_overflow_24h` counted a feed that was all new after a gap: Dukagjini, re-enabled after hours auto-disabled, 50 of 50. It now counts only fetches one run after the previous one (≤ 1.5 scheduler intervals); the others are in `details.after_gap`. What remains is real: Indeksonline, Lajmi.net, Zëri, BalkanWeb and Kallxo published more than the 10 items their feeds list between two runs on 16–17 Nov, so items were missed.
  - `source_freshness_6h` was right: Gazeta Express (HTTP 403 to automated clients, also from GitHub's runners) and Ekonomia Online (404; the feed moved to `/sq/rss.xml`) have been auto-disabled for days. It now names them with the last error and last success.
  - A run ended `partial` for any failed fetch: one `http_error` of Euronews Albania, or the 6-hourly probe of an auto-disabled source. It is now `partial` when a source fails `SOURCE_FAILURE_PARTIAL_THRESHOLD` (3) fetches in a row, every source is down, or on an outage on this side; the public status is `degraded` from the same 3 failures.
  - The run's log and its annotations on the Actions page, and the `dq:` alert, now carry the check's one-line summary (`source_freshness_6h: not collected: gazeta-express auto-disabled (http_error: HTTP 403; …)`), not only its name.
- **Gazeta Express is disabled** (`enabled: false`). It has answered HTTP 403 to automated requests for days, to the free pipeline on GitHub's runners and to the *Source validation* runs alike, and was auto-disabled after 20 failures in a row. Disabled in sources.yaml it is no longer probed every 6 hours nor reported by `source_freshness_6h`; when the weekly validation shows its feed working again, set `enabled: true`.
- **Ekonomia Online is collected again.** Its feed answered 404 and the source had been auto-disabled; the site now links `https://ekonomiaonline.com/sq/rss.xml` (50 items), which the *Source validation* workflow found and verified. The new `feed_url` enables it again at the next sync.
- **`enabled: false` in sources.yaml now wins over an auto-disable**, as documented. An auto-disabled source kept its auto-disable, so it was probed every 6 hours, re-enabled if a probe worked, and `source_freshness_6h` warned about it for good. And a new `feed_url` for an auto-disabled source enables it again at the next sync (its failures were the old URL's), instead of waiting up to 6 hours for a probe.

### Added
- **A busy outlet's items are no longer missed between two runs.** Most feeds list their latest 10 items; on 16–17 Nov Indeksonline, Lajmi.net, Zëri, BalkanWeb and Kallxo published more than that between two 30-minute runs, so items fell out of the feed unread. When every item of a fetch is new although the source was fetched before, ingestion now reads the feed's next pages (`?paged=2`, `?paged=3`, WordPress style) until they reach items already stored. Their items count towards that fetch, so `feed_overflow_24h` warns only where that did not catch up or the feed has no next page. A next page that fails is not a failure of the source. The *Source validation* report has a *Page 2* column saying which feeds have one.

### Changed
- **Today's news gets the model before last month's.** The model's queue put every article `enrich-requeue` sent back (the workflow's *reanalyse*: thousands of rule-based analyses from weeks back) ahead of the day's provisional rule analyses, so on 13–17 Nov the free quota went to old news and not one of the day's articles was upgraded (`queued_upgrades=0` in every run). Articles without any analysis still come first; after them, every article waiting for a better analysis ranks by date, newest first.
- **A free model's daily quota is spread over the day** ([ADR-033](docs/DECISIONS.md#adr-033-a-free-models-daily-quota-spread-over-the-day-and-learned-from-its-429s)). Gemini's free quota ran out at about 19:00 UTC, because the first runs after its midnight reset (Pacific time) sent requests as fast as the queue allowed; the evening's and the night's news then got the keyword rules. Each model now sends per run what is left of its day divided by the runs left until the reset (at least one request; the last run before the reset takes what is left), newest articles first; when a run's share is used (`quota_capped`) the fallback model takes the next articles within its own share, and the rules the rest. New settings, per model: `LLM_REQUESTS_PER_DAY` / `LLM_FALLBACK_REQUESTS_PER_DAY` (0 = learn it), `LLM_MAX_REQUESTS_PER_RUN` / `LLM_FALLBACK_MAX_REQUESTS_PER_RUN` (a fixed ceiling, 0 = none), `LLM_QUOTA_TIMEZONE` (`America/Los_Angeles`) / `LLM_FALLBACK_QUOTA_TIMEZONE` (`UTC`). The figure is only a start: a 429 that names a daily limit marks the model limited, later runs send one request before any other, and a day that ends limited teaches the real limit; a day with more requests and no 429 raises it; a 429 per minute changes nothing. Counts are kept in `ops.llm_quota` (migration 0008). A 429 for the day is no longer retried. The run's stats add `requests`, `request_cap`, `quota_capped`, `rate_limit_scope` and `quota`. Steps and the GitHub variables to set, in Albanian: [NISJA.md](docs/NISJA.md#2-ai-falas-pa-server-faqja-online-pjesa-b).
- **A look of its own: the printed page** ([ADR-032](docs/DECISIONS.md#adr-032-a-look-of-its-own-the-printed-page)), replacing the Ground News look (ADR-028). A serif nameplate whose ë's dots are two squares of ink, one section line between rules, warm newsprint and ink; headlines in a 30 KB subset of Source Serif 4 with size-matched fallbacks; perspective in brick / light neutral / blue, the only split drawn in hue (region and ownership in one ink); coverage bars that leave a blindspot's side as an empty slot and print counts; a newspaper's grid on desktop and, on a phone, the first headline in the first screen with the controls folded to one line. The story page puts who covered it and the headline comparison first. New favicon and link-preview image. No page scrolls sideways on a phone any more (a blindspot's sentence used to widen the home page and outlet profiles), and a test keeps it so.
- **No first reporter when the times cannot tell.** "Reported first" went to the earliest timestamp, but an article whose feed gives no usable time carries the time it was first *seen*, up to a fetch interval after it was published. When another outlet's report has such a time within 30 minutes of the earliest one, the story now names no first reporter (`first_source_slug` and `first_source_name` are null, no article has `is_first`), and the outlets' first-report counts follow the same rule. An estimated time that is itself the earliest still counts: the article was published even earlier.
- **GitHub's fallback schedule no longer repeats a run.** Two clocks start the free pipeline (cron-job.org, and GitHub's schedule as a fallback, ADR-029). A scheduled start now does nothing when a run that did not fail started within 20 minutes (`gjurme scheduler --once --skip-if-recent 20`): no second fetch of every feed and no extra database wake-up. Runs started by hand or by the outside clock always run.

### Added
- **Several articles in one request** (`LLM_BATCH_SIZE`, `LLM_FALLBACK_BATCH_SIZE`; 5 for the first model on the free path, 1 elsewhere). Free tiers count requests a day and the instructions are about thirty times the size of one article, so Gemini's 250–300 requests now cover close to five times the articles. Every article's analysis is still validated and grounded against its own text and stored on its own; an article missing from a batch answer, or a whole answer that cannot be used, is asked for again article by article (`batches`, `batch_fallbacks` in the run's stats). *Free model quality* has a `batch_size` input to measure it.
- **The keyword rules' analysis is provisional** (`ENRICH_RULES_UPGRADE_HOURS`, 48 on the free path). The rules now step in at the end of the run in which no model analysed an article instead of 60 minutes later, so the newest reports reach their stories in the run that fetched them rather than 1.5–2 hours after publication. For 48 hours the article stays queued for a model, after the articles without any analysis, and a model replaces the rule-based analysis when its quota allows; before, a rule-based analysis stayed until a manual `reanalyse`.

### Changed
- **The front page ranks coverage against age.** "Most covered" counted outlets alone, so the biggest story of the last three days led the page until it left the range, up to two days after its last report. The count is now halved for every third of the range since the story's latest report (24 h for the feed's three days, at least 12 h): yesterday's ten-outlet story ranks with today's five-outlet one, and a month still ranks by coverage. Blindspots use the same order.

### Fixed
- **A broken fallback model no longer goes unnoticed.** Alerts and the run's status read only the first model's stats, so with Gemini's quota used up and Groq's key revoked a run ended `succeeded` and the keyword rules analysed everything. A fatal error or open circuit breaker of the fallback model now alerts (`llm_fallback_fatal_error`, `llm_fallback_circuit_open`) and ends the run `partial`. A new quality check, `rule_based_share_24h`, warns when the rules analysed more than half of the last 24 hours although a model is configured, and alerts above 90 %. `enrichment_failure_rate_24h` no longer counts a used-up quota (429) as a failure.

### Added
- **A fallback when a free model's quota runs out.** On 2026-11-05 Gemini's free tier ran out after about 250–300 requests, while the outlets publish several hundred articles a day. A second model (`LLM_FALLBACK_*`, e.g. Groq's free tier) now takes over for the rest of the run when the first one stops early, and any article no model analysed within `ENRICH_RULES_AFTER_MINUTES` (60 on the free path) gets the disclosed keyword-rule analysis instead of none. A used-up rate limit no longer trips the circuit breaker. *Free model quality* can test the second model (`fallback_key`, `response_format`). `LLM_REASONING_EFFORT` / `LLM_FALLBACK_REASONING_EFFORT` send `reasoning_effort` to reasoning models such as Groq's `openai/gpt-oss-120b` (Groq's Llama models are Enterprise-only since 2026), so their thinking leaves room for the JSON.

### Changed
- **Unchanged API answers revalidate with `304 Not Modified`:** every public answer carries a weak `ETag`, so a returning reader's browser downloads only what changed.
- **Stories wait for the analysis:** an article is clustered once its analysis succeeded or was given up, or after `STORY_ENRICHMENT_WAIT_MINUTES` (6 h); the name guards that keep look-alike events apart read the analysis.
- **Before any outlet is rated** (the launch state), coverage is split by region: lenses with nothing to show leave the pickers, story pages say no outlet is rated yet, the Blindspot page explains the missing left/right lists and My News counts regions.
- **Faster story pages:** story queries read only the days a story can span (home feed 182 → 57 ms, story page 130 → 20 ms, source profile 656 → 172 ms at 365k articles), and blindspots are found in SQL over every story of the range, not the 2,000 biggest.
- **`/api/v1/status`** is cached for 30 s, counts with indexes (80 → 12 ms), reads "degraded" while a source is auto-disabled and "stale" when none is collected.
- **Auto-disabled sources** are fetched again every 6 h and re-enabled on success.
- **CI:** every action is pinned to a commit SHA and gitleaks to a digest (after the trivy-action tag hijack, CVE-2026-33634); the image job logs in to GHCR only after the vulnerability scan.

### Fixed
- Every story, outlet and topic page told search engines it was a copy of the home page (`<link rel="canonical" href="/">` in the shared `index.html`). Canonical links and `og:url` are now set per page.
- A missing story, outlet, topic, person or article showed the raw API message, and an invalid id (`/stories/abc`) loaded forever; both show a "not found" page marked `noindex`.
- A few hours without network on the server auto-disabled every source for good, and the system then reported itself healthy. A run in which 75 % or more of 3+ sources fail is treated as an outage on this side and not counted.
- One feed item with a NUL character made the whole fetch unstorable, silently; control characters are dropped and a fetch that cannot be stored is logged and counted.
- Random query parameters (`?x=<random>`) bypassed the response cache, and a client under the rate limit could exhaust the database pool; the cache key is now the endpoint's declared parameters. Unexpected errors carry the request id and security headers.
- A hand-run `gjurme enrich`/`cluster`/`process` overlapping a scheduled run failed the stage on a duplicate analysis; stage commands take the pipeline lock and a concurrent result is retried.
- Excerpts of articles whose analysis failed for good were never purged (90-day retention); `enrichment_backlog_6h` warned forever about such articles.
- Backups never ran on a bootstrapped server (the directory was not writable by the backup container's UID 70) and every failure path was silent. Backups alert first, from an exit trap, and check writability at start; `rclone sync --max-delete 4` keeps an emptied directory from wiping the off-site copy.
- Rolling back a release that added a migration failed silently; `gjurme db upgrade` now leaves a database migrated by a newer release as it is.
- `.env.example` comments after empty values became the values in Compose (`IMAGE_TAG='# leave empty…'`).
- A story listed twice on one page gave two headings the same id.
- An open tab could not recover after a deploy: Caddy answered a missing code chunk with `index.html`, cached as immutable for a year, and the app had no error boundary. Missing chunks are now a `404`, the app reloads once and otherwise explains, and the HTML of every route revalidates.
- The keyword-rule placeholder (`[headline, no AI summary] …`) was shown as a summary in article lists.
- Form control borders had 1.75:1 contrast (WCAG asks 3:1); now ≥ 3.4:1.
- Four tests started failing on runs after 8 October: the feed fixtures carry fixed September dates that PROCESS now rejects as too old. `fixture_bytes(fresh=True)` moves them forward by whole weeks.
- Keyword rules: location and organization hints written with `ë`/`ç` (*Shqipëri*, *Lëvizja*) never matched, because names are compared in folded form; the hints are now folded too.
- Manual `docker compose` commands on the server (the `dc`/`gj` aliases, backup, restore, off-site) failed with "IMAGE_TAG must be set"; they now read the running tag from `.deploy-state/current`.
- Server bootstrap no longer enables UFW on Oracle Cloud Ubuntu images, which Oracle warns can prevent boot. It opens 80/443 in Oracle's own iptables rules instead (tested for idempotency and with `iptables-restore --test`).
- The keyword-rule "summary" is labelled `[headline, no AI summary]` instead of `[demo heuristic]`.
